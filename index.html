Who We Are

About Alberto

Alberto Watch Company is a full-service watch repair, appraisal and retail store offering the finest selection of luxury timepieces with first-class service since 1962.

We carry brand-new watches from Michael Kors, Rolex, Citizen Eco-Drive, Bulova and more, alongside meticulously curated vintage pieces and our own Alberto house collection.

Founded in Lagos and now with branches in Abuja, Port Harcourt and Kano, we are Nigeria's premier destination for luxury timepieces. Our certified watchmakers service everything from antique pocket watches and grandfather clocks to the latest generation smartwatches.
